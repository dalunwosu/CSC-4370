About ShopSmart

Know before you go.

What ShopSmart Does

ShopSmart is a shopping list app. You build a list, set your preferred brands once, and from then on ShopSmart takes it from there. It searches grocery stores in your area, pulls current prices and active discounts, and shows you the total cost at each store side by side, so you know exactly where your list is cheapest before you ever leave the house.

The app remembers your brand preferences after the first time you set them, and if a store doesn't carry a specific item, it suggests a comparable alternative, like a Kroger-brand swap when the item isn't available at Walmart, so your list is never left incomplete.

Our Story

ShopSmart started from a familiar, frustrating moment: standing at the checkout line, bags in hand, only to find out later that the same items were cheaper a few miles away. That small sting of "I could have saved money" is what pushed me to build something that removes the guesswork from grocery shopping altogether.

This project sits at the intersection of two things I care about: writing software that solves a real, everyday annoyance, and thinking like a founder about how a small idea could grow into something people actually rely on.
