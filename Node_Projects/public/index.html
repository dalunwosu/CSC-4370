From list to cheapest store in three steps

You do the setup once. After that, a new list takes a minute.

  1. Build your list

    Add what you need and pick the brands you prefer. ShopSmart remembers them next time.

  2. Choose how far to go

    Set a radius. ShopSmart finds the stores inside it and checks which ones carry your items.

  3. Compare the totals

    See each store's total with discounts applied, then head to the one that costs least.

Built around the way you actually shop

Small things that stop a grocery run from turning into a guessing game.

  • Per-store totals

    Your whole list priced at every nearby store, side by side.

  • Discounts included

    Active deals and coupons are counted, so the total matches what you would pay.

  • Saved brand preferences

    Set your brands once and skip re-entering them on every list.

  • Swaps for missing items

    If a store doesn't carry something, ShopSmart suggests a comparable item instead of leaving a gap.
