The Team Today

Liverpool's first team brings together experienced leaders, creative midfielders, quick attackers, and younger players developing at the highest level. The squad competes in English and European football throughout the season.

Virgil van Dijk provides leadership in defence, while players such as Alexis Mac Allister, Dominik Szoboszlai, Ryan Gravenberch, and Florian Wirtz offer different qualities in midfield.

The attacking group includes players such as Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo, Hugo Ekitike, and Federico Chiesa. Squad membership changes through transfers and academy promotions. (The squad picture for this season isn't out yet so this is for last season)
