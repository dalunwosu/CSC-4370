Built Through Generations

Liverpool FC began in 1892 and gradually became one of England's leading clubs. Bill Shankly's arrival in 1959 helped transform the team, establish a winning culture, and begin a new era at Anfield.

Success continued under managers such as Bob Paisley, Joe Fagan, Kenny Dalglish, Rafael Benítez, and Jürgen Klopp. Their teams produced league triumphs, domestic cups, and famous European victories.

Liverpool's official honours list now records 52 major men's trophies. That total includes a record-equalling 20 English league titles and six European Cups, the most won by a British club.
